## Why this one is twice as long

⚠ **Three axes multiply, they do not add.** 3 brands × 3 colours × 2 lengths = **18 items**, so 17
clones — not 8 — and 622.72s of footage against the other two recipes' ~380s. Nothing about the
method changed; the arithmetic did.

**The clone loop is an odometer: the last axis spins fastest.** Brand holds still, colour is the
middle, length runs to the end each time. So the number of dropdowns that move per clone varies, and
the footage shows all three cases:

| clones | dropdowns moved | when |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | one | the length alone |
| 6 | two | the colour steps and the length resets |
| 2 | **three** | the brand steps — **items 7 and 13** |

Nothing declares that. It falls out of comparing each item to the row before it. **Items 7 and 13
are the moments worth cutting to** if the video needs to show the pattern changing.

⚠ **The lengths are stored as text with the unit inside the value** — `"115 cm"`, `"120 cm"` — which
is how this store's five existing "Mens Ski Poles" collections already store them. Two lengths and
not three is the only reason this is 18 items and not 27.

⚠ **The colours are the one invented set.** This store has no colour axis anywhere, so Black, Red
and Blue were chosen — deliberately plain, because the colour is there to prove a third axis, not
to be interesting.

## Known caveat

⚠ **Use `narration_report.json`, not `script.json`'s `estimated-word-time`.** The latter is
`words / 3.44` and has no term for punctuation; `say` pauses at every comma. The report holds the
measured length of every line. None is rushed — all play at 155 wpm.
