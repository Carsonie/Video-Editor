## What is in it

Nine items — three brands × three sizes — and **only the first is built from scratch.** The other
eight are BCP's own **Duplicate**, renamed, with the one dropdown that differs re-picked, then
Update.

⚠ **Duplicate does NOT save what is in the form.** duplicate → edit → Update, never the other way.

⚠ **The sizes are 6, 7 and 8, which is US shoe sizing and not mondo.** That is this store's own
scale — its existing "Mens Ski Boots" collection runs 6 to 8.5 — and the recipe follows the store
rather than correcting it. Do not "fix" it in the narration.

⚠ **The brands are the same three as the skis**, on purpose. All three make boots, and a package
whose skis and boots offer one brand list is the point of the package.

## The words

21 scenes, **20 spoken and 1 silent**, 451 words. The lines are the skis' lines with the nouns
changed — they are generated from one source so they cannot drift apart.

⚠ **Scene 18 is silent on purpose, and it is 157.8 seconds long.** Carson, 2026-09-10: "add the
voice for every scene, except for the the items, since they are all repeats of the same first
item." The eight clones are a single scene with no cue, as long as the footage it covers. The first
item's closing line carries it.

## Nothing was stretched

0 screens short; `stretch_report.json` says `stretched_any: false`. Recorded at a flat minimum
first, screens measured off the delivered mp4, lines written to fit. `built` is the real screen
length and can be trusted as screen edges.

## Known caveat

⚠ **Use `narration_report.json`, not `script.json`'s `estimated-word-time`.** The latter is
`words / 3.44` and has no term for punctuation; `say` pauses at every comma. The report holds the
measured length of every line. None is rushed — all play at 155 wpm.
