HOW intro-picklist.png WAS MADE
===============================
Built 2026-10-08 for the picklist video's 00-intro bookend.

⚠ THE PNG IS NOT IN GIT, AND CANNOT BE. `Customers/**` is excluded and only
.json / .md / .txt / .vtt are un-ignored under help-videos. So this file IS the
asset as far as the repo is concerned: the picture is reproducible from the
command below, byte for byte, and nothing else records the step list.

(special-skis' intro PNG looks like an exception. It is not — it sits in
TOOLS/ and was tracked before the rule existed, and a tracked file stays
tracked whatever .gitignore later says.)

THE COMMAND
-----------
    cd "Customers/Rentify Demos Corp/ski-demo/help-videos/TOOLS"
    python3 make_intro.py \
      --w 2304 --h 1926 \
      --title "The Picklist" \
      --sub "Use and Review" \
      --step "Sign in" \
      --step "Find your store" \
      --step "Opening the order picklists" \
      --step "Filtering by days out" \
      --step "Opening a picklist to print" \
      --step "Marking a picklist ready" \
      --step "Sign out" \
      --out "../BCP/picklist/0_master/intro-picklist.png"

⚠ THE TITLE IS ONE LINE, AND THAT IS NOT A STYLE CHOICE. A two-line title —
"The Picklist|Use and Review" — makes make_intro.py REFUSE:

    the intro needs 1744px but only 1698px fits
    — drop a step, shorten the title, or lower the sizes

It measures the layout instead of letting it overflow. Seven steps and a
two-line title do not both fit in 2304x1926. One line frees ~170px and keeps
all seven steps, which is the trade that was taken.

⚠ THE STEPS COME FROM THE FLOW, NOT FROM MEMORY. They are the eleven numbered
steps of
    Basic_E2E_Testing/Master_Flows/BCP/Nav/flows/picklist_review.test.ts
grouped into seven a viewer would read. If the flow changes, reread it rather
than editing this list from recall — the whole point of a "what this covers"
card is that it matches what the film actually shows.

ENCODING IT AS THE BOOKEND
--------------------------
    ffmpeg -y \
      -loop 1 -framerate 25 -i 0_master/intro-picklist.png \
      -f lavfi -i anullsrc=channel_layout=stereo:sample_rate=48000 \
      -t 4 -r 25 -fps_mode cfr \
      -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 18 \
      -c:a aac -b:a 128k -shortest \
      2_scenes/sandbox/00-intro/segment.mp4

⚠ THE SILENT AAC TRACK IS REQUIRED, NOT COSMETIC. 38 of picklist's 39 scenes
carry audio, and a clip with none in a concat drops or shifts the sound of
everything after it. special-skis' bookend carries one for the same reason.

⚠ 00-intro HAS NO ROW IN script.json, ON PURPOSE. That is what makes it a
bookend: the SAE's `in_script` flag already understands a scene folder with no
script row, and giving it one would put it in the narration and the VTT.

⚠ AND CHECK THE LAST FRAME, NOT THE FIRST. A `-loop 1` that ends early looks
perfect at frame 1. The check that caught nothing here but would catch that:
extract at -sseof -0.1 and diff against the source PNG — mean difference came
back 0.19/255, which is just h264 being lossy.
