# ski-demo — add-item (BCP admin)

**Purpose: VIDEO SEED** — raw footage for a help/promotional video, to be finished in the Video-Editor repo. Not a bug repro.

This folder holds **eleven takes of the same flow**, recorded as one set. Takes 1-4 are **look
tests** — a progression comparing the click-highlight style (fill vs. border-only, ring-through-
typing, rounded corners) — and stay exactly as filmed; none are re-takes of a failure, all eleven
passed, all eleven are kept. Take 5 added narration-minimum screen holds (`SCREEN_MIN_MS`) on top of
take 4's look. Take 6 added a 750ms nav highlight, per-box PIN entry, and automatic lead-trim.
Take 7 added a 1500ms nav highlight, a typed item description, and an item image — but the image
landed LAST in the field order (after price/qty/requirement were already saved) and was only
partially in frame (a sliver at the top edge). Take 8 fixed all three of those: ring-only
click-highlight became the standing default, the pointer parks off after every navigation click
(no leftover tooltip), and the image now uploads FIRST — right after the item shell is created and
before any of its fields are filled. Take 9 added three further, automatic fixes aimed at three
things Carson spotted in a frame of take 8: the pointer now arrives at a target BEFORE the ring
lights it, so a tooltip named for that action opens together with the ring instead of after it's
gone; the item's image Dropzone rings while the image loads, plus ~1.2s after, since nothing is
clicked to upload it; and the Ctrl+K palette's "Logout" row is meant to ring for ~0.9s before Enter
is pressed. The first two were confirmed working in take 9's file. The third was not — the Logout
row never rang in take 9, and its final login-screen hold was cut to a fraction of a second instead
of the intended ~4.6s. Take 10 carried take 9's look and both working fixes forward, plus two more
attempts aimed squarely at what take 9 got wrong — an opposite-corner pointer park, and a longer,
SELECTED-state Logout ring — and **neither closed its issue**: the Store Lookup leftover tooltip was
unchanged (~0.9s), and the Logout row still never rang, with take 10's tail-trim cutting its ending
even harder than take 9's (the file ends mid-Ctrl+K-palette, before the login screen is ever reached
at all). **Take 11 fixes the tail-trim itself** — `record_flow.ts`'s automatic tail cut is now OFF
by default (it was guarding against desktop-bleed that has never once occurred in ten takes across
five recipes), so take 11's closing login-screen hold survives intact: **≈2.4s**, matching the
intended ~2s, against take 9's under-half-a-second and take 10's zero (it never got there). **But
the Logout row still does not ring in take 11 either** — confirmed by an exhaustive per-frame scan
(dense, sub-pixel-stride, both full-frame and palette-region-restricted) of the entire window from
the moment the Update-button ring clears to the moment the palette closes: zero yellow pixels, at
every single frame, with no "no Spotlight row found to ring" warning in the log either — the same
shape of silent non-render as takes 9 and 10, on a third independent recording. **Take 11 is still
the current one to cut the video from** — it is strictly ahead of every earlier take (every fix
through take 9 confirmed still working, plus the intact closing hold that takes 9 and 10 both
lacked) — but an editor pulling the Logout moment itself should know **no take in this folder has a
ringed Logout row**; see Take 11 and Known Issues below. Takes 1-8 do **not** have take 9's
pointer-before-ring or Dropzone-ring behaviour — don't cut from them expecting it — and takes 1-6
never show a description field or an image at all.

`CLICK_HIGHLIGHT_FILL`, `TYPE_HIGHLIGHT` and `CLICK_HIGHLIGHT_RADIUS` are all new
(`Master_Flows/Core/lib/clickPace.ts`, added 2026-09-09). `CLICK_HIGHLIGHT_FILL` only changes
the fill wash inside the highlight ring. `TYPE_HIGHLIGHT` changes what happens to the ring
after a click that starts typing: normally (take 1, take 2) the ring is drawn 250ms before the
click and cleared the instant the click lands, so the characters that follow appear in an
unmarked field. Under `TYPE_HIGHLIGHT=1` (takes 3 and 4) the click's ring is NOT cleared — it is
parked and inherited by the typing on that same field, so selecting and filling a field reads as
one continuous ring instead of a blink-then-nothing. It clears when the typing finishes, and any
parked ring is cleared before the next click. `CLICK_HIGHLIGHT_RADIUS` (take 4 only, takes 1-3
were filmed before it existed) gives a 6px corner radius to the ring **only if the element being
ringed is itself square to begin with** — an element that already has its own radius (an input,
a pill button) keeps exactly what it has. The yellow border itself is identical across all
four takes (3px logical / 6px real-pixel #FFD400 ring), same 250ms-before / 500ms-after click
timing, same everything else.
