# ski-demo — add-item (BCP admin)

**Purpose: VIDEO SEED** — raw footage for a help/promotional video, to be finished in the Video-Editor repo. Not a bug repro.

This folder holds **five takes of the same flow**, recorded as one set. Takes 1-4 are **look
tests** — a progression comparing the click-highlight style (fill vs. border-only, ring-through-
typing, rounded corners) — and stay exactly as filmed; none are re-takes of a failure, all five
passed, all five are kept. **Take 5 is the one to cut the video from.** It is take 4's look
(border-only, ring held through typing, rounded corners) plus one further change: every screen is
now held on camera for at least as long as Sarah's narration for that screen needs
(`SCREEN_MIN_MS`), because on take 4 seven of the twelve screens ran shorter than their scene —
12.1s of missing footage in total. Takes 1-4 do **not** have this and are not narration-timed;
don't cut from them expecting the dwell times to match a script.

`CLICK_HIGHLIGHT_FILL`, `TYPE_HIGHLIGHT` and `CLICK_HIGHLIGHT_RADIUS` are all new
(`Master_Flows/Core/lib/clickPace.ts`, added 2026-09-09). `CLICK_HIGHLIGHT_FILL` only changes
the fill wash inside the highlight ring. `TYPE_HIGHLIGHT` changes what happens to the ring
after a click that starts typing: normally (take 1, take 2) the ring is drawn 250ms before the
click and cleared the instant the click lands, so the characters that follow appear in an
unmarked field. Under `TYPE_HIGHLIGHT=1` (takes 3 and 4) the click's ring is NOT cleared — it is
parked and inherited by the typing on that same field, so selecting and filling a field reads as
one continuous ring instead of a blink-then-nothing. It clears when the typing finishes, and any
parked ring is cleared before the next click. `CLICK_HIGHLIGHT_RADIUS` (take 4 only, takes 1-3
were filmed before it existed) gives a 6px corner radius to the ring **only if the element being
ringed is itself square to begin with** — an element that already has its own radius (an input,
a pill button) keeps exactly what it has. The yellow border itself is identical across all
four takes (3px logical / 6px real-pixel #FFD400 ring), same 250ms-before / 500ms-after click
timing, same everything else.

## What's on screen (all five takes, identical script)
1. Login screen (2s hold), email verification / auth code entry
2. Click-through nav via admin's own buttons: Businesses card -> pick "Rentify Demo Corp" -> Lookup Stores -> pick "Ski Demo - Blue Mountain" -> gear -> Lookup Items -> "+"
3. New Item form: name typed in, collection searched/selected ("Mens Ski Boots"), requirement selected, variant (Quality Mens Ski Boots) selected, item saved (price $25.00, qty 25)
4. Logout, 2s hold on login screen

One continuous browser window throughout each take — one login, one logout, no desktop bleed.
Click targets highlight yellow ~250ms before each click and hold 500ms after, per house style.
Takes 3, 4 and 5 additionally keep that ring lit through the typing that follows a click on the
same field. Takes 4 and 5 additionally round the ring's corners to match a square element's own
6px radius (see Take 4 above) while leaving already-rounded elements untouched. Take 5 alone adds
the per-screen minimum dwell time (`SCREEN_MIN_MS`) so the footage has enough runway for
narration — see Take 5 above for the twelve screen-by-screen holds.
